## `src/assets/fonts/DejaVuSans.ttf`

- **Source package:** `dejavu-fonts-ttf@2.37.3` (DejaVu Fonts 2.37)
- **Purpose:** Unicode TrueType font bundled for official PDF payment receipts (`src/lib/receipt-pdf.ts`), supporting full Cyrillic (Russian & Cyrillic Uzbek, e.g. `Раҳмат`) and Uzbek Latin diacritics/apostrophes (`Oʻ`, `Gʻ`, `U+02BB`, `U+02BC`).
